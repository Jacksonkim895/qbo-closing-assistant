QBO Closing Assistant

An internal QuickBooks Online integration operated by Seokhyun (Jackson) Kim

QBO Closing Assistant is a private, non-commercial tool that connects to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's official API. It is used by the operator in the course of his own professional accounting work to:

  • Retrieve financial reports (trial balance, general ledger, profit & loss, balance sheet) for month-end and year-end closing review;
  • Record recurring and adjusting journal entries;
  • Create customer invoices from prepared schedules.

The tool runs locally on the operator's computer. It is not distributed to the public, is not listed on the Intuit App Store, and is not offered to third parties.

How connections work

A QuickBooks Online company is connected only when its administrator explicitly authorizes this application through Intuit's standard OAuth 2.0 consent screen. The connection can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps → Connected apps).
