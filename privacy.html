Privacy Policy

QBO Closing Assistant · Effective September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QBO Closing Assistant (the "Application"), operated by Seokhyun (Jackson) Kim (the "Operator"), handles information obtained from QuickBooks Online ("QBO") companies that are connected to it.

1. Who operates the Application

The Application is a private, internal tool operated by the Operator, an individual accounting professional, for use in his own professional work. It is not a commercial product and is not made available to the general public.

2. Information the Application accesses

When a QBO company administrator authorizes the Application, it may access the following data from that company through Intuit's QuickBooks Online Accounting API:

  • Company information and chart of accounts;
  • Transactions, including journal entries, invoices, bills, payments and related records;
  • Customer, vendor and item lists;
  • Financial reports such as trial balance, general ledger, profit & loss and balance sheet.

The Application does not access payroll data, bank login credentials, or payment card information.

3. How the information is used

Accessed data is used solely to perform accounting and bookkeeping tasks for the connected company, such as month-end closing review, recording journal entries and preparing invoices. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the accounting services being performed.

4. Where the information is stored

  • The Application runs locally on the Operator's computer. It does not operate a server or cloud database of its own.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Intuit are stored in a local configuration file on the Operator's computer and are used only to communicate with Intuit's API.
  • Reports and working files generated during closing work may be saved locally on the Operator's computer and in the Operator's professional file storage, subject to the same confidentiality obligations that apply to the underlying accounting engagement.

5. Sharing of information

The Operator does not sell, rent, or share QBO data with third parties. Data is disclosed only (a) to the connected company itself or persons it has authorized, (b) where required by law, or (c) to service providers that process data on the Operator's behalf under confidentiality obligations, such as the AI assistant service used to analyze reports and prepare entries. Such providers do not receive QBO login credentials or OAuth tokens.

6. Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are retained only while the connection is active. When a company disconnects the Application, its tokens become invalid and are removed from the Operator's configuration. Working files are retained in accordance with applicable professional record-keeping requirements and are deleted thereafter.

7. Security

Access to QBO is obtained exclusively through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow; the Application never receives or stores QuickBooks passwords. Configuration files containing tokens are kept on an access-controlled computer and are not transmitted by email or placed in shared folders. Deletion of QBO transactions through the Application is disabled.

8. Your choices

A connected company can revoke the Application's access at any time in QuickBooks Online under Settings → Apps → Connected apps, or by contacting the Operator. Revocation takes effect immediately.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
